# On Cue Privacy Policy

_Last updated November 26, 2026_

On Cue is a class timer for instructors on iPhone and Apple Watch. It doesn't collect, sell or share
any of your information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

## What stays on your device

Your classes, blocks, exercises, settings and class history are stored only on your iPhone and Apple
Watch. On Cue doesn't send them to me or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

## Apple Health

On Cue starts a workout session so your class timer and its cues keep running while your phone is
locked. It doesn't read or save any Health data, and nothing about your workouts leaves your device.

## Notifications

Cues on your Lock Screen are created on your device. They don't pass through any server.

## Sharing classes

When you share a class or block, On Cue creates a file that you send with AirDrop, Messages, Mail or
Files. It goes only to the people you choose. The file contains the class or block, not your tags or
teaching history. I never see it.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
